Dear Nowsin,

If I could write a letter to describe what you mean to me, I would need infinite pages. But I will try to capture a fraction of these feelings in the imperfect language of words.

You are the unexpected gift I never knew I needed. The answer to prayers I didn't know I was praying. From the moment you entered my life, everything changed quietly — like the first light of dawn, so gentle you almost don't notice, until suddenly the whole world is brighter.

Your laugh is my favorite music. Your presence is my favorite place. Your love is the most beautiful feeling I have ever had the privilege of knowing. When I am with you, I am the best version of myself — and that is the greatest gift one person can give another.

I love not just who you are, but who I become when I am around you. You make me want to be kinder, braver, more honest. You make me believe that the world is genuinely good because you exist in it.

So here is my promise, written in starlight and kept in the deepest part of my heart: I will love you through the easy days and the hard ones. Through every version of you and every version of me. I choose you — not just today, but in every tomorrow I am given.

Forever yours, Siam ✨
